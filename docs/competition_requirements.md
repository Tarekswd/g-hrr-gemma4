# Competition Requirements & Constraints: Research Track

## Official Competition Rules
1.  **Target Subject**: Methodologies, empirical studies, or architectures advancing autonomous software engineering agents utilizing the **Gemma 4** open-weights family (specifically `gemma-4-31b-it-qat-w4a16-ct`).
2.  **Word Limit**: Maximum 3,000 words (excluding references and appendices).
3.  **Scientific Ethics**:
    *   No synthetic or fabricated experimental results.
    *   Empirical claims must be backed by transparent methodology and statistical reporting (confidence intervals, sample sizes).
    *   All citations must refer to authentic, published scholarly works.
4.  **Key Deadlines**:
    *   Paper Track Submission: November 12, 2026 (23:59 UTC).
    *   Evaluation by Google DeepMind research committee.
    *   Top papers invited to present at the Google Expo at NeurIPS 2026.
